Core Advisory Practices

Structured across four core message territories, anchored by deep diagnostic capability in agriculture, supply chains, and industrial manufacturing.

1. Business Performance & Diagnosis

Diagnose what is limiting the business and fix what matters. We deploy deep operational assessments on processing floors, distribution routes, and management hierarchies to eradicate cash leaks and margin decay.

2. Capital Readiness & Investment

Strengthen businesses so they are positioned for appropriate institutional financing. We align accounting systems, debt-service capacity, board oversight, and legal structures before market approach.

3. Market Access & Trade

Connect viable productive capacity with commercially verified domestic and international demand. We structure aggregation models across agricultural hubs and industrial corridors.

4. Strategic Transactions

Facilitate strategic opportunities, asset disposals, joint ventures, and off-take partnerships where LMG maintains explicit advisory mandates and execution capabilities.
